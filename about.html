Who I am

I am a 17 year old web developer based in Bulgaria who creates simple and clean websites that will help your small business convert visitors into clients. I create responsive websites that are mobile and user friendly, keeping in mind your objectives as a business. When you hire me, you will be working with the same person who will be creating your website, there is no intermediary involved. I will take the time to understand your vision and bring it to life through your website.

Delivery timeline

Your time is valuable for me and I will make sure that your website is completed on schedule. The specific deadline depends on what the website will offer, however, I will always keep you informed about your progress.

What I need from you

I will require some information about your business such as the name and the type of content that you want on the website. If you have any design ideas, color schemes, favorite websites, or any particular features in mind, do not hesitate to let me know.
